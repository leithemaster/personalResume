Questions

Are you available? Yes. I'm looking for a front-end developer role where design and engineering sit close together: front-end, UI or design engineering.

How do you work with teams? Early and in the open. I run feedback sessions with stakeholders, bring work to design review before it's polished, and sit in sprint planning so design decisions arrive with their cost already known. When I hand off, it's to people I've been talking to all along.

Do you design or develop? I'm a developer first, with years on the design side too, and on most projects one leads. At Geocent I led the design and handed it to developers. At Sev1Tech I built the features and shaped the design along the way.

What tools do you use? React, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and Angular on the front end, React Native for mobile, Adobe XD for design. Spring Boot and PostgreSQL behind it. GitHub, GitLab, Docker and Jira to ship, in Scrum.

Do you use AI tools? Yes. I build with Claude Code as a pair programmer. I write the spec, it drafts, and I review every change against the design and against WCAG before it ships. This site was made that way.

How do you handle accessibility? As structure. I review against Section 508 and WCAG while designing, so contrast, focus order and semantics are settled in the plan instead of patched after launch.

Can I see code? My public work is on GitHub. Client work stays with the client, so the case studies use redrawn figures.
